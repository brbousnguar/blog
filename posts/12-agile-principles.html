Quick Brief

12 Principles
6 min Read time
Infographic Primary format

Published February 25, 2026

Audience: Executive and delivery leadership Use case: Team kickoff and alignment sessions

Use the infographic first, then skim these compact notes.

  1. Deliver value early and continuously. Release small, useful increments so customers get benefit quickly and can steer priorities.
  2. Welcome changing requirements. Treat change as an advantage, especially when it helps the customer compete or learn faster.
  3. Deliver working software frequently. Short cycles reduce risk, expose issues early, and keep progress tangible.
  4. Business and developers collaborate daily. Tight collaboration prevents misalignment and speeds decisions.
  5. Build around motivated individuals. Trust the team, give them support, and let them own solutions.
  6. Prefer face-to-face communication. Rich, direct communication avoids misunderstandings and keeps flow smooth.
  7. Working software is the primary measure. Real outcomes matter more than documents or plans.
  8. Sustainable pace. Avoid burnout so quality stays high and delivery remains predictable.
  9. Technical excellence and good design. Strong engineering practices make change cheaper and faster.
  10. Simplicity. Do the least that solves the problem and avoid unnecessary complexity.
  11. Self-organizing teams. The best solutions emerge when teams decide how to do the work.
  12. Regular reflection and adjustment. Retrospectives keep the team improving their process and results.
